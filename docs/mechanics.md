The left PCB foundation extends from floor to board and joins the wall. A small relief clears an underside component. Right ledges bridge over the servo; no screw holes pass through the motor space. Left screws secure the PCB and the right cover pads limit upward movement.

All four cover screw blocks join adjacent walls, with necessary servo/clamp clearance. The broad clamp retains the simplified servo case envelope. Display and button openings have been restored to full cover thickness; their cut-through cylindrical fingers are replaced with rectangular pads.

The user's actuator placeholder and shifted rear window are preserved. Window height was previously reduced 20% from 32 to 25.6 mm. The reference switch is 86 mm square; real rocker dimensions, travel and faceplate curvature remain unverified. The preserved shielding overlaps that reference rocker; see validation.
